About

Experienced accounting discipline, now available remotely.

For more than nine years, I’ve worked in accounting and finance operations — reconciling bank accounts, processing supplier payments, managing receivables, preparing payroll, handling petty cash, and supporting month-end activities.

In my current role, I reconcile 18 company bank accounts each month and prepare weekly payroll for 32 production employees. That experience taught me to resolve discrepancies early, keep documentation complete, and make sure the numbers can be followed back to their source.

I now bring that discipline to remote bookkeeping. My focus is simple: clean records, clear communication, dependable reconciliation, and practical systems that reduce repetitive work without removing human review.
