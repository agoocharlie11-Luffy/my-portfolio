From a Gmail PDF to a reviewed Xero draft.

A working n8n prototype that uses Gemini for invoice extraction and explicit accounting checks before a draft reaches Xero.

Verified in a test organisation · 12 November 2026

The problem

Typing supplier invoices and checking their totals takes time. Automating entry still needs controls for missing fields, duplicate bills and accounting review.

What I built

Gmail label intake and PDF upload, Gemini extraction, validation, supplier and duplicate checks, Xero draft creation, and a persistent review table.

How it behaves

  1. Choose an invoice: apply the Gmail label Xero-Invoices, or upload one PDF through the form.
  2. Extract and validate: Gemini returns structured fields. Checks cover required values, customer, dates, line arithmetic, subtotal, tax and total.
  3. Check Xero: match the configured supplier and look for an existing bill with the same invoice number.
  4. Draft or review: a supported, mapped invoice becomes a DRAFT. Unsupported or ambiguous invoices are saved for manual review.
  5. Record the outcome: save the result and add Xero-Processed to the email. The next regular run excludes it.

Current demo scope

  • One PDF per email or upload, up to 10 MB.
  • Hourly Gmail check; one email per run.
  • PHP and explicit zero tax.
  • One explicitly mapped fictional supplier and demo expense account.
  • Unknown suppliers and unsupported cases require review.

Human review stays in place

The workflow creates drafts only. It does not approve invoices or make payments. Account coding comes from an explicit mapping, not an AI guess. A real deployment needs client-approved contacts, accounts, taxes, permissions and representative tests.

How I would demonstrate it

Show the successful Gmail execution, compare the extracted fields against the fictional PDF, open the two-line Xero draft, and then show the duplicate-detection result and review record.
